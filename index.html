Strategic ABM Execution

Strategy & Approach

  • Multichannel ABM: LinkedIn Sponsored Content + InMail to C-suite, custom nurture sequences with personalized case studies, programmatic retargeting on target domains

CAC Reduction Through Testing

12-Week Experiment 26% Improvement
Data-Driven Optimization

The Hypothesis

Structured A/B testing across creative, audience, and landing page variables would identify higher-performing combinations to reallocate budget toward, reducing overall CAC by 20%+.

Test Matrix

  • Creative Format: Static images vs. Short-form video + carousel → Video won (+37% CTR)
  • Audience Targeting: Broad interest-based vs. In-market intent + competitor lookalikes → Intent-based won (-23% CPA)
  • Landing Page: Generic company site vs. Dedicated vertical hub with social proof → Hub won (+41% conversion)
Metric Before After
Cost Per Lead (CPL) $142 $105
Landing Page Conversion 8.3% 19.4%
Campaign ROI 3.8x 6.1x

Scalable Processes Established

Created experimentation calendar with pre-defined test types and owners. Implemented "learn and kill" framework for underperforming campaigns.
